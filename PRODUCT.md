# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
1. Creative Directors, Producers, and Agency Clients seeking high-tier video editing, storytelling, and motion grading.
2. Tech Leads, Engineering Managers, and Recruiters hiring senior Big Data Engineers for distributed data pipelines, lakehouse architectures, and cloud streaming systems.
3. The Portfolio Owner who actively manages work items, skills, inquiries, and personal biography via the custom in-app admin portal.

## Product Purpose
Provide an immersive, dual-identity digital portfolio that credibly demonstrates mastery in two distinct high-skill fields (Video Editing and Big Data Engineering), backed by a fully dynamic Appwrite CMS and custom admin panel.

## Positioning
Unlike conventional single-focus portfolios or disjointed sub-sites, this platform bridges cinema-grade visual storytelling with rigorous distributed systems engineering under a coherent, high-craft interactive experience powered by modern Appwrite backend architecture.

## Operating Context
Evaluated on desktop by creative agencies reviewing video cuts and engineering teams inspecting architecture schematics, as well as on mobile by busy recruiters. Managed on desktop or mobile by the owner via secure Next.js and Appwrite credentials.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Track switching between `Cinema Reel` (dark cinematic video theater, timeline scrubbers, YouTube playback) and `Data Engine` (pipeline blueprints, benchmarks, system metrics).
- Split-Screen Gateway on landing with persistent switcher.
- Full CRUD capabilities for Showcase Items, Skills, Profile, and Contact Inquiries via a custom Admin Dashboard.
- Fast streaming via embedded YouTube players within custom modal shells.
- Next.js 16 (App Router) with Tailwind CSS v4, Base UI, Lucide icons, and Appwrite Node/Web SDKs.
- Graceful fallbacks and seed data when Appwrite credentials are being configured during initial launch.

## Brand Commitments
- Dual visual world: Cinematic Dark with filmic accents for Cinema Reel; High-Density Cyber-Technical Precision with telemetry styling for Data Engine.
- Production-grade polish adhering to Impeccable principles (no generic templates, purposeful micro-interactions, distinct typography hierarchy).

## Evidence on Hand
- Next.js 16 project structure with Tailwind CSS v4 already initialized.
- Seed data for both Video Editing (showreels, documentary edits, commercials) and Big Data Engineering (Kafka streaming, Lakehouse pipeline, Spark clustering).

## Product Principles
1. **Show, Don't Claim**: Video items lead with immersive playback; Data items lead with quantifiable impact metrics and pipeline topologies.
2. **Frictionless Dual Identity**: The two tracks must feel like native masterclasses in their own right, never compromised compromises of each other.
3. **Autonomous Control**: Every string of text, project, metric, and skill must be editable by the owner without touching code.
4. **Performance First**: Lazy loading of media, instant track transitions, zero playback lag.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigable track switcher and theater modals, high contrast text ratios, reduced motion fallbacks for cinematic transitions, accessible ARIA labels for interactive schematics.
