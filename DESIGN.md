# Design System: Notion Warm Paper & Amber Line-Art

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Design Philosophy

A warm, intellectual, and refined technical aesthetic inspired by the iconic **Notion Design System**, engineered specifically for a **Staff Big Data & Distributed Systems Engineer**. Combines warm paper surfaces (`#FBFBFA`), deep carbon ink typography (`#37352F`), Notion's signature warm amber/orange and golden yellow accents (`#D9730D` & `#DFAB01`), and high-craft animated line-art architecture SVGs without corporate sterile glare, dark mud, or generic badge clutter.

## Typography Stack
- **Headings**: `Outfit` (`var(--font-heading)`) — clean, geometric presence in Notion carbon ink `#37352F`.
- **Body & Architecture Notes**: `DM Sans` (`var(--font-body)`) — high-legibility sans-serif with natural reading rhythm in Notion grey `#787774` and deep ink `#37352F`.
- **Telemetry & Metadata**: `JetBrains Mono` (`var(--font-mono)`) — tabular numerals, code stamps, and real-time readouts (`350K+ msg/s`, `< 38 ms P99`, `8.6 PB`).

## Palette & Surface Strategy

### Foundations
- **Canvas Base**: Notion Signature Warm Paper `#FBFBFA` with subtle amber/orange atmospheric radial spotlight glows and minimalist micro-grid overlay.
- **Surface Elevation**: Crisp White Cards `#FFFFFF` and Notion Callouts `#F7F6F3` with 1px delicate borders (`rgba(55, 53, 47, 0.12)`) and soft Notion elevation (`box-shadow: rgba(15, 15, 15, 0.04) 0px 0px 0px 1px, rgba(15, 15, 15, 0.03) 0px 2px 4px`), elevating on hover with a warm orange border lift (`border-[#D9730D]/45`).
- **Typography Base**: Notion Carbon Ink `#37352F`, secondary Notion grey `#787774`, and muted metadata `#9B9A97`.
- **Primary Accent**: Notion Warm Orange (`#D9730D`, hover `#C26207`, tint `#FAEBDD`) for stream conduits, live metrics, primary CTA, and active selections.
- **Secondary Accent**: Notion Amber Yellow (`#DFAB01`, hover `#CB9018`, tint `#FBF3DB`) for distributed compute cores, stateful CEP engines, and rotating telemetry rings.
- **Tertiary Accent**: Notion Slate Blue (`#0B6E99`, tint `#E7F3F8`) for Lakehouse storage and repo links.
- **Telemetry Indicators**: Notion Forest Green (`#0F7B6C`, tint `#EEF5F2`) for cluster health and zero-loss guarantees.

## Anti-Habit Checklist (Strictly Enforced)
- ❌ No harsh, blinding stark white or cold hospital-gray backgrounds.
- ❌ No pill badges or rounded-full badge containers.
- ❌ No kicker / eyebrow tags above headings; typography commands authority directly.
- ❌ No static, boring clip-art; real living vector geometry with animated conduit flow in Notion line-art style.
- ❌ Zero defects reported by `impeccable detect`.
